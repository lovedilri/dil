Privacy Policy

Dofu Live NFL Football · Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what Dofu Live NFL Football (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android application SportStream (package com.fetch.skjstudios.live.score), published by SKJ Studios.

The short version. We do not ask you to create an account and we never ask for your name, email, phone number or address. The app uses Google Firebase for analytics and notifications, and CAS.AI for advertising. These services collect device and usage information as described below.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

None. Dofu Live NFL Football has no sign-up, no login and no profile. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, contacts, calendar, files or location.

Information collected automatically

WhatCollected byWhy
App events — screens opened, matches viewed, streams started, session length Google Firebase Analytics To understand which features are used so we can improve the app
Device information — model, operating system version, language, country, screen size Firebase Analytics, CAS.AI Compatibility, crash diagnosis, and correctly sized ads
Crash and performance diagnostics Google Firebase To find and fix bugs
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier, not tied to your identity) CAS.AI and its mediation partners To show ads, limit repeats, and measure ad performance
Push notification token — an anonymous identifier for your app installation Firebase Cloud Messaging To deliver match alerts and app announcements
IP address Our content servers and advertising partners Standard for any internet request; used for approximate country and fraud prevention

2. Advertising

Dofu Live NFL Football is free and is supported by advertising. Ads are served through CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions), which works with several advertising networks, including Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network and others.

These partners may use your advertising ID and device information to select and measure ads. You control this identifier from your device: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset it or turn off personalised advertising entirely. Turning it off does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant.

Partner policies: CAS.AI · Google · AppLovin

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we may send you alerts about matches and app updates. Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your installation an anonymous token so messages can be delivered. This token is not linked to any personal information.

You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings under Apps → SportStream → Notifications.

4. Content in the app

Dofu Live NFL Football organises and links to sports content hosted by third parties. We do not host, encode, record or broadcast any video ourselves. When you open a stream, your device connects directly to that third-party server, and that server’s own privacy practices apply. We have no control over what those servers collect.

All trademarks, team names and logos belong to their respective owners. Dofu Live NFL Football is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any league, team, or broadcaster.

5. How we use information

  • To show live scores, schedules and results
  • To keep the app working and diagnose crashes
  • To understand which features people use, so we can improve them
  • To display ads, which keep the app free
  • To send notifications, if you have allowed them

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Google Firebase — analytics, crash reporting, notifications, remote configuration
  • CAS.AI and its advertising partners — advertising and measurement

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to investigate fraud or abuse of the app.

7. Data retention

Analytics data is retained by Google according to the settings on our Firebase project, typically between 2 and 14 months. Advertising identifiers are handled under each partner’s own retention policy. Notification tokens are removed automatically when you uninstall the app.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID — reset or opt out in Settings → Google → Ads
  • Notifications — turn off in your device’s app settings
  • All app data — uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from your device
  • Deletion request — email us and we will ask our providers to delete data associated with your installation

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a US state with a privacy law such as California, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete information held about you, and to object to certain processing. Contact us using the address below and we will respond.

9. Children

Dofu Live NFL Football is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app is configured so that its advertising partners treat users as a general (non-child) audience. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
